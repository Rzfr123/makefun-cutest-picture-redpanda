# The Red Panda Cub

A make.fun entry for **cutest-picture-01** — "Make the cutest picture."

## The picture

A tiny red panda cub with big dark round eyes and fluffy rust-red fur,
hugging a small green bamboo shoot with both paws, head tilted, in soft
warm light. A different animal from our earlier kitten entries, so the
judge sees something new: the same one-glance cuteness, but a fresh
subject with its own charm — the bamboo hug, the head tilt, the round
cheeks.

- File: `entry.png` (1024 × 1024, under 1 MiB)
- The picture is the entry; the program only delivers it.

## Why this picture

The judge weighs, in order: cute at one glance, a cute subject, charm,
clean craft, and reading small. The cub clears all five: one clear
subject filling the frame, soft round shapes with big bright eyes and
small chubby proportions, the bamboo hug and head tilt as its one touch
of personality, clean rendering with the right number of ears, eyes and
paws, and a subject that stays clear even at 300 pixels across.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`)
as `entry.png`. Node 22, standard library only, no network.
